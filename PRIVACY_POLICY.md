# Privacy Policy
**Last updated:** 
November 2, 2026

This policy explains what Cytosine records when you use it, why, and what it leaves alone. 
By adding Cytosine to a server or using any of its commands, you agree to this policy.

> [!CAUTION]
> This page contains AI-generated text. It was reviewed and edited before publishing, but if anything here is unclear or looks wrong, contact us and we'll fix it.
>
> We are against selling user data. Cytosine does not sell it, and we will not start.

## 1. What we record

Every time someone runs one of Cytosine's official commands, we save it to our database. That includes:

- The command that was run.
- The query, meaning whatever was typed into the command (for example, the song or radio station you searched for).
- The IDs and timestamp needed to tell where and when the command was run.

## 2. What we do not record

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### We do not record:
>
> - Your messages. Cytosine does not store what you say in chat.
> - Anything that happens outside the bot's official commands.
> - Auto-moderation activity. The auto-moderation system checks messages as they come in, but nothing it scans is saved.

If it is not a command and its query, it is not in the database.

## 3. Why we record commands

We record commands for two reasons:

- **Testing and error hunting.** Cytosine is a hobby project, and logs show what broke, when, and what was typed to break it.
- **Moderation.** Logs let us look into abuse of the bot, such as spamming commands, exploiting the economy, or breaking our [Terms of Service](README.md).

We do not use this data for anything else.

## 4. Who can see it

Only the developer of Cytosine can access the database. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or hand it to third parties. The only exception is if we are legally required to disclose something, or if Discord asks us to as part of a Terms of Service investigation.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep command logs for as long as we need them for testing and moderation. If you ask us to delete your data, we will.

## 6. Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below. If you want to stop all collection, stop using the bot's commands, or remove Cytosine from your server.

## 7. Children

Cytosine is for people who are allowed to use Discord. Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or the minimum age in their country). We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If you think we have, tell us and we'll remove it.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when. If you keep using the bot after a change, you accept the new version.
