## 1. Information we record

We record two categories of information.

### 1.1 Command records

Each time an official Cytosine command is run, we save a record of it to our database. That record contains:

- The command that was run.
- The query, meaning whatever was entered into the command (for example, a song or radio station that you searched for).
- The identifiers and timestamp needed to determine where and when the command was run.

### 1.2 Auto-moderation violation records

When the auto-moderation system detects a violation, we record the type of violation that was committed, together with the identifier of the user concerned and the time of the violation. We use these records to calculate the Global Score described in Section 5.

## 2. Information we do not record

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### We do not record:
>
> - The content of your messages. We do not store what you say in chat.
> - The message in which an auto-moderation violation occurred, or any text, attachment, or other context surrounding it. Only the type of the violation is retained.
> - Any activity that takes place outside the Bot's official commands and the auto-moderation system described in this policy.

## 3. Why we record information

We record information for the following purposes only:

- **Testing and error detection.** Cytosine is a hobby-scale project. Command records show us what failed, when it failed, and what input caused the failure, which allows us to correct defects.
- **Moderation and abuse prevention.** Command records allow us to investigate misuse of the Bot, including command spam, exploitation of the economy, and breaches of our Terms of Service.
- **Operation of the Global Score.** Violation records allow us to calculate and maintain the Global Score, which is intended to assist server owners and moderators.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling for commercial purposes, or any purpose unrelated to the operation, safety, and improvement of the Bot.

## 4. Auto-moderation

The auto-moderation system is an instrument that assists server owners and moderators. It is not a judge, a jury, or an executioner. It does not decide guilt or impose punishment of its own accord, and it is not a substitute for human judgment. Any action taken in a server on the basis of its output is the decision and responsibility of that server's owner or moderators.

The system examines messages in order to detect violations. It does not retain those messages. Once a violation has been detected, the only data kept is the type of violation, as described in Section 1.2.

## 5. The Global Score

### 5.1 How it works

Each user has a score (the "Global Score"). When a user commits a violation, their Global Score is reduced by an amount that we determine, based on the type of violation. We may change the amounts, and the way the score is calculated, at any time.

### 5.2 Global distribution

The Global Score is distributed globally. This means it is associated with the user rather than with a single server, and it is made available across the servers in which Cytosine operates. A violation committed in one server may therefore affect your Global Score in all of them.

### 5.3 Restoration

A reduced Global Score is restored after thirty (30) days during which the user has committed no further violation, whether or not the user has otherwise been active. Each new violation may delay restoration. We retain violation records for as long as we reasonably need them to operate the Global Score.

### 5.4 Limits

The Global Score is an automated indicator and may be inaccurate. We provide it as is, without any warranty that it is correct, complete, or suitable for any particular decision. It must not be treated as conclusive evidence of wrongdoing. If you believe your score is wrong, you may contact us using the details in Section 12.

## 6. Server owner controls

Server owners may disable the auto-moderation and Global Score feature for their server. When it is disabled, violations in that server are not recorded and the server does not use the Global Score. Disabling the feature does not remove violation records that were created before it was disabled, and it does not affect records created in other servers.

## 7. Security and encryption

We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect the information we store. All stored data is secured and encrypted. Access to the database is restricted to the developer of Cytosine. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Who can access your information

Only the developer of Cytosine has access to our database. We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it with advertisers or other third parties. The Global Score is shared only to the extent described in Section 5. We may disclose information where we are required to do so by law or by a valid legal process, or where Discord requests it as part of an investigation into a violation of its terms.

## 9. How long we keep information

We keep command records for as long as we need them for testing, error detection, and moderation. We keep violation records for as long as we reasonably need them to operate the Global Score. We do not retain information indefinitely without reason, and we delete information upon a valid request as described in Section 10.
